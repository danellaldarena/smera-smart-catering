# SMERA – Smart Catering Platform

**Smart Catering for Smart People**

## Overview

SMERA is a statistics-driven smart catering platform designed to connect consumers with catering SMEs (*Small and Medium Enterprises*) in Surabaya, Indonesia. The platform aims to help students, university staff, and busy individuals plan healthier, more varied, and more convenient meals while supporting food businesses through data-driven operational management.

SMERA addresses common challenges such as difficulty choosing daily meals, repetitive menus, irregular eating habits, unpredictable food demand, and inefficient inventory management.

By integrating meal planning, personalized menu recommendations, nutritional insights, customer analytics, and stock forecasting, SMERA aims to create a more efficient and sustainable digital food ecosystem.

## Project Objectives

- Help consumers plan their daily and weekly meals more conveniently.
- Provide varied menu recommendations based on user preferences and nutritional goals.
- Connect consumers with local catering businesses through a digital platform.
- Help catering SMEs understand customer preferences and purchasing patterns.
- Improve inventory planning and reduce food waste through demand forecasting.
- Encourage the digital transformation of local catering businesses.

## Key Features

### 1. Anti-Boredom Menu Generator

Provides diverse menu recommendations and reduces repetitive meal suggestions by considering users' previous choices and menu characteristics.

**Proposed methods:**
- Clustering to group menus based on cuisine, ingredients, and nutritional characteristics.
- Rule-based recommendation to manage menu rotation and avoid excessive repetition.

### 2. Smart Health Integration

Helps users plan meals according to their daily nutritional goals, including calories, protein, and carbohydrates.

**Proposed methods:**
- Nutritional data modeling.
- Linear optimization to identify meal combinations that approach users' nutritional targets.

### 3. Customer Insights & Preference Prediction

Helps catering vendors understand customer segments, purchasing behavior, and popular menu combinations to support marketing and menu planning.

**Proposed methods:**
- K-Means Clustering for customer segmentation.
- Market Basket Analysis to identify frequently ordered menu combinations.

### 4. Automated Stock Forecasting

Helps catering vendors estimate future ingredient requirements based on historical sales and demand patterns.

**Proposed method:**
- ARIMA time series forecasting to analyze demand trends and support inventory planning, including consideration of seasonal factors.

## Target Market

SMERA focuses on two main user groups:

**Consumers**
- University students aged 18–25 in Surabaya.
- Students living in boarding houses (*kos*).
- University lecturers and campus employees.
- Busy individuals seeking convenient and varied meal options.

**Catering Vendors**
- Home-based catering SMEs.
- Food stalls and catering businesses around university campuses.
- Catering providers serving campus events.

## Market Research Highlights

The initial research described in the proposal reported that:

- 82.4% of 74 consumer respondents had difficulty choosing daily meals.
- 81.1% expressed a need for meal-planning support.
- 89.2% were interested in an application for catering selection and food-demand prediction.
- 83% of the surveyed catering businesses expressed interest in stock management and customer analytics.

These findings indicate potential demand for an integrated platform serving both consumers and catering vendors.

## Business Model

SMERA proposes several revenue streams:

- **Transaction commissions:** A fee from catering orders placed through the platform.
- **Premium subscriptions:** Additional personalized meal-planning and nutritional features.
- **Vendor subscriptions:** Access to customer analytics and stock forecasting tools.
- **Institutional and B2B partnerships:** Catering services for universities, organizations, and campus events.
- **Advertising and brand partnerships:** Promotional opportunities for relevant food and health-related businesses.

## Technology and Analytical Methods

The proposed analytical approaches include:

| Feature | Method |
|---|---|
| Menu grouping | Clustering |
| Menu recommendations | Rule-based recommender |
| Nutritional planning | Nutritional data modeling and linear optimization |
| Customer segmentation | K-Means Clustering |
| Menu combination analysis | Market Basket Analysis |
| Stock forecasting | ARIMA time series forecasting |

The final technology stack and implementation details depend on the development stage of the application.

## Project Status

SMERA is presented as a smart catering business concept supported by market research, proposed statistical methods, business strategy, and UI/UX prototypes.

The repository can be expanded as the project progresses to include application development, data analysis notebooks, forecasting models, and interactive dashboards.
